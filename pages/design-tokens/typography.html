Usage Guidelines

Text Hierarchy

  • Headings: Use heading tokens for consistent hierarchy
  • Body Text: --ds-text-base with --ds-leading-relaxed
  • Small Text: --ds-text-sm for captions and labels
  • Display Text: --ds-text-4xl or larger for hero sections

Font Weight Usage

  • Light (300): Large display text only
  • Regular (400): Body text and paragraphs
  • Medium (500): Subtle emphasis
  • Semibold (600): Headings and buttons
  • Bold (700): Strong emphasis and titles
